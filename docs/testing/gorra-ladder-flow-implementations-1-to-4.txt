GORRA LADDER FLOW — MANUAL TEST CHECKLIST
IMPLEMENTATIONS 1 TO 4

A. MODE + WORKSPACE
1. Open Ladder A.
2. Switch Individual to Bulk.
3. Create a local Bulk draft.
4. Open Ladder B.
5. Return to Ladder A.
6. Reload the browser.

Look for:
- The chosen mode is remembered globally.
- Ladder A draft remains with Ladder A.
- Ladder B does not receive Ladder A draft.
- Meaningful drafts survive reload.
- Search text, temporary drag state, and modal state do not unexpectedly restore.

B. INDIVIDUAL / BULK PLAYER CONFLICT
Individual: select Player A. Switch to Bulk and try to use Player A.

Look for:
- Player A is unavailable.

Then create a Bulk draft with Player B and return to Individual.

Look for:
- Player B is unavailable.
- Local selection alone does not write to the backend in Network.

C. INDIVIDUAL PLAY LADDER MATCH
Choose an eligible pair and click Play Ladder Match.

Look for:
- Exactly one Challenge and one Match are created.
- The Match status is READY, not LIVE.
- A scorer is not assigned merely because the match was created.
- Opening View Match does not start it.

D. INDIVIDUAL SCHEDULE
Schedule a future date and time.

Look for:
- One canonical Match with SCHEDULED status.
- Correct date, time, and court when one was chosen.
- The match appears in Play.
- Refreshing does not create a duplicate.

E. BULK PLAY
Create several Bulk pairs and use Play Ladder Match on one.

Look for:
- The selected pair becomes READY.
- The successful pair is removed appropriately.
- Unrelated Bulk pairs remain.
- A failed request keeps the pair for retry.

F. BULK SCHEDULE
Schedule one Bulk draft.

Look for:
- One canonical Match with SCHEDULED status.
- Only the successful draft is removed.
- Unrelated drafts remain.
- Retrying does not duplicate the Match.

G. RETRY / DOUBLE CLICK
If practical, simulate a retry or double submit.

Look for:
- One canonical Challenge and one canonical Match.
- The same IDs are returned for the same logical request.
- No duplicate Play rows.

H. PLAY — NORMAL PLAYER
Sign in as a participating player.

Look for:
- Scheduled, ready, and live matches appear.
- Unrelated Ladder matches do not appear.
- There is no three-match cap.

I. PLAY — CLUB ADMIN / CO-ADMIN
Sign in as a Club manager.

Look for:
- Active Club Ladder matches appear even when the manager is not a participant.
- Explicit records from another Club do not appear.

J. SCHEDULED MATCH ACTIONS
Participant: View match.
Manager: View match, Reschedule, Cancel.

Look for:
- Start match is not shown while the match is merely scheduled.

K. READY MATCH ACTIONS
Participant: Start match, View match.
Unrelated manager: View match, Reschedule, Cancel.

Look for:
- An unrelated manager cannot Start Match merely because they manage the Club.

L. START READY MATCH
As a participant, click Start match.

Look for:
- Status changes READY to LIVE only after the click.
- The Match ID stays the same.
- No second Match appears.
- LiveMatch opens.

M. LIVE SCORER
As the current scorer, look for Resume scoring and open it.

Look for:
- Score state is preserved.
- The same Match ID is used.
- There is no restart or reset.

N. LIVE PARTICIPANT — NOT SCORER
Look for View live score and open it.

Look for:
- A read-only scoreboard.
- No scoring authority is granted merely by viewing.

O. LIVE CLUB MANAGER
With live-control permission, look for View live score and Open match control. Open control.

Look for:
- Opening control does not silently replace the scorer.
- The same canonical Match remains.

P. RESCHEDULE
Manager reschedules an eligible match.

Look for:
- The same Match ID.
- Updated future time and court when changed.
- The Play list updates without a full browser reload.
- No duplicate row.

Q. CANCEL
Manager cancels a match.

Look for:
- Confirmation.
- The canonical record becomes cancelled.
- It disappears from the active Play list.
- No replacement Match is created.

R. ACTIVE CLUB ISOLATION
Switch the active Club.

Look for:
- The Play list changes to that Club.
- Explicit other-Club Ladder matches never leak across.

S. NETWORK / PERFORMANCE
During mode switching, search, drag, and local draft selection, inspect Network.

Look for:
- No unnecessary backend writes.
- Backend calls only for real operations such as create, schedule, reschedule, cancel, and explicit start.
- No obvious request loop.
- No repeated duplicate requests caused by rendering.
