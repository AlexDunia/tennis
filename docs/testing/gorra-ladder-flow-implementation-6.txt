GORRA Ladder automatic completion — browser checklist

1. Start a READY Ladder Match and score until the tennis engine declares a winner.
2. Finish the match. Confirm the same Match ID shows Completed with its final score, no Pending Review,
   no opponent-confirmation message, and no optional Ladder-update prompt.
3. Open Ladder. Confirm W/L and matches played update; configured position-swap or leapfrog movement is
   visible immediately; completed players are available again; and eligible opponents use the new ranks.
4. Open Play. The completed Match is absent from active operational matches.
5. Open Match Details. Confirm Completed, final score, court, format, and the Ladder updated note.
6. Retry the completion if practical. Confirm no duplicate movement, activity, wins, losses, or matches.
7. Open a seeded legacy pending_review record. Confirm its existing review/confirmation flow still works.
